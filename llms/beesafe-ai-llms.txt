# Beesafe AI

> Beesafe AI is a fraud-prevention and anti-scam platform — "Stopping Scams Before They Reach Your Customers." Its AI-powered anti-scam agents proactively engage scammers to uncover the fraudulent accounts, money-mule networks, and criminal financial infrastructure behind trust-based scams (romance scams, pig-butchering, investment fraud). It serves financial-services, cryptocurrency, telecommunications, and government customers. Backed by Y Combinator, Obvious Ventures, and SBIR/STTR. Currently early access with no public self-service API.
